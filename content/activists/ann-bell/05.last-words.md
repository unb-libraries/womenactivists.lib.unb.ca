When asked how she would like to be remembered, Ann replied, "kindly." She recognizes that everyone will know and remember a person differently:

> The only way you can influence others is how you live your life everyday. I've seen a number of generations come and go in my own family, and it's not being remembered that interests me; what interests me is what I can do today.

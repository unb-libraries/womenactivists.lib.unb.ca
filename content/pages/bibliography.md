Any bibliography is out of date as soon as it is issued but this one in 2011 will direct you to reputable books and further references. Reputable journal articles would be available via searches in university library data bases such as Academic Search Premier. Use this list as a base for exploring a rich history of principled activities, especially regarding Canadian experience.

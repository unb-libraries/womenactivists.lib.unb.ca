When Liz asks Phyllis how she would like to be remembered, her answer is simple:

> Mostly, I think, going back to the values my parents taught, to be remembered as someone who worked for a fairer world, one where the enormous privileges that I enjoy are more equally shared around; to balance kindness with honesty; and as someone who was always willing to step up to the plate.
